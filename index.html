FAQ

How do we get there?

Elm House is about 50 minutes' drive from Brisbane, up Mount Glorious Road. The last stretch is narrow and winding, so allow extra time and take it slow after dark.

Can I just get an Uber home?

Please don't count on it. Rideshare up the mountain is unreliable, so sort out a lift or a designated driver before the day.

What will the weather be like?

Cold. August nights on Mount Glorious drop well below Brisbane, and mist often rolls in. Dress warm and bring a jacket.
